A compact inline sequence or equivalence chain — plain pill boxes linked by a connector glyph. Lighter weight than StepPipeline (no numbers, no body text) — use for a short derivation or equivalence, not a multi-sentence process.

```jsx
<Chain chapter={5} items={['Conjoint', 'Consider', 'Jointly']} connector="=" />
<Chain chapter={1} items={['MaxDiff scores', 'Binary top-choice data', 'TURF bundle']} connector="→" />
```
